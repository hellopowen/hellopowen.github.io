Before you write

Arts Council England's short Guidance for Design letter writers is the one document worth five minutes first. Everything on this page follows it.

Each letter writer also receives a personal brief, prepared for them and sent directly. This page is the shared factual half of it, kept here so it is always current. If you were expecting a brief and do not have it to hand, email Po-Wen at hellopowen@gmail.com and he will resend it.

The record

The venture timeline
  • Sep 2016Joins Philips Experience Design Shanghai as product design consultant.
  • 2017Initiates and leads design of the Intelligent Mask intrapreneurial venture: user-need research, experience workshops, benchmarking, product-system architecture.
  • 2018First-generation Fresh Air Mask development; led the Product Design Language System for Philips Air quality and Homecare categories in 2018.
  • 2019Fresh Air Mask Series 6000 launches commercially; served as one of the global lead designers for the Global Domestic Appliances Product Design Language System in 2019.
  • Mar 2020Leaves Philips.
  • Oct 2020Dutch Design Week 2020. Philips presents the Fresh Air Mask; the official page lists “Philips Fresh Air Mask — Po-Wen Shih, Yuping Cheng & Hans Han”; the documentary names Po-Wen as Senior Product Designer.
  • 2020–2022The family he founded continues: Kids Fresh Air Mask in 2021 and Fresh Air Mask Lite in 2022 launch and receive international recognition. Both launched after his March 2020 departure — they are the lineage of the programme he founded, not his authorship of those variants.
Product and programme recognition

All recognition below is given to the product, to Philips, or to Philips Experience Design — not to any individual. Po-Wen's connection is his founding or lead design contribution, witnessed by colleagues. Please don't describe any of it as a personal award.

  • Fresh Air Mask familySuccessful Design Award (CN) 2020, Most Successful Design, Series 6000 · DFA Design for Asia Merit 2020 · iF Design Award 2020 · iF 2021, Red Dot Winner 2021, DFA Merit 2021 and Successful Design Award 2021 for the Kids Fresh Air Mask · Red Dot Best of the Best 2022 for the Fresh Air Mask Lite. Credited to Philips Experience Design; Po-Wen Shih served as founding designer for the Fresh Air Mask programme.
  • Air Purifier — Red Dot Best of the Best 2018Philips 2-in-1 Air Purifier and Humidifier received Red Dot Best of the Best 2018, with Po-Wen Shih named in public acknowledgement by Philips Chief Design Officer Sean Carney. Po-Wen Shih is named in the Red Dot Design Yearbook 2018 as one of four designers of the awarded product, Philips Design Shanghai. That is the awarding body naming the design team — not a personal award to anyone, and the page carries no face captions, so it names the team rather than who is who.
  • Air Purifier — iF Design Award 2018Philips 2-in-1 Air Purifier and Humidifier received iF Design Award 2018, credited to Philips Design / Philips Design Air team, during Po-Wen's tenure at Philips Experience Design Shanghai as lead designer for the Philips Air quality Product Design Language System.
  • Air Purifier — iF Design Award 2020Philips Air Purifier Series 3000i/4000i received iF Design Award 2020, credited to Philips Experience Design; contribution-era programme recognition, announced after Po-Wen's March 2020 departure. Please don't describe it as recognition received during his role.
  • Domestic AppliancesiF Design Award 2022 for the Philips All-in-One Cooker 3000 Series, credited to Philips. Po-Wen's contribution is the 2019 advanced-development design work behind it, not the delivered production programme: he set the usability of the exhale and steam pressure-release system, and the front taper that made room for the full-function enlarged front interaction panel — together the core design archetype the product carries. He was not the sole designer of the Cooker, and the 2021 Multi-purpose Cooker is a different product.

The mask records are held by Koninklijke Philips N.V. The air-purifier and interface records are recorded to Versuni Holding B.V., the company formed from the Philips Domestic Appliances business. A change of holder is an administrative event and does not affect who is named as creator.

Three of these registrations have effect in the United Kingdom. DM/205058 holds granted design protection in the United Kingdom. DM/202563 and DM/202564 name the United Kingdom as their sole designated territory, and are registered and renewed in the United Kingdom, no refusal recorded. Po-Wen is named as their creator, not their holder, and where a design is filed is the holder's commercial decision, not a connection of his — a letter should not present it as one.

UK context, for the two mandatory paragraphs

Every letter must say how Po-Wen would benefit from living in the UK, and how he would contribute to UK design and cultural life. Factual basis, in your own words:

  • Where he isHe lives and works in the UK now, in Reading, working in Bicester, at the intersection of British automotive design and engineering culture and hardware–software product experience.
  • Benefit to himThe UK's design-engineering ecosystem — automotive, consumer hardware, design consultancies — and its design institutions offer networks and scale his practice cannot access elsewhere.
  • His contributionTwenty years of international product-design and design-language-system experience brought into UK teams; upstream product-definition capability bridging design, engineering and human factors; mentoring and design-community participation.
  • Future plansContinuing UK-based product-design leadership, UK design-community engagement, and publishing his design-system thinking. He can share more on request.
