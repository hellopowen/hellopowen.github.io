How the credit reads

Every entry below carries its own credit line, so the wording is already right where you find it.

The recognition went to the products, to Philips and to Philips Experience Design. What Po-Wen contributed is the founding and product-design work behind those products, and that is the part colleagues saw at first hand. The design registrations name him personally, and so does the Red Dot Design Yearbook 2018, which lists him as one of four designers of the awarded product. Those are the two kinds of record here that name a person rather than an organisation. They are public, dated and self-proving — the full list is below, and it is the shortest way to see the authorship for yourself before you write a word.

His practice began in industrial and product design and widened from there: to design strategy and the design language system, then to the range built on that system, then to designing for a new business, then to whole-product definition. Each step kept the one before it. A letter about his craft and a letter about his strategy are letters about the same designer.

In his own words: “In most product organisations one group decides what to build and another decides how it gets built, and the distance between them is where a product loses its point. I work in that distance. Knowing how a thing is actually made is what earns the right to decide what should be made.”

Design-language-system programmes

A product design language system is a strong, dynamic guideline that helps the brand experience be perceived, be felt, and be a benefit at every touchpoint a customer meets. It is dynamic because three things move: the brand, the customer and the context.

  • Philips Air / Homecare, 2018Led the Product Design Language System for Philips Air quality and Homecare categories in 2018. Urban Living was the pilot range. He worked with business, with engineering, with research and with the larger design team to investigate the next generation of product, and ran the workshops that produced three themes and synthesised them into one set of principles. The destination was better air and better quality of life.
  • What it becamePhilips launched the Urban Living range in October 2020, after his departure. Red Dot’s 2021 citation for the AC3998 purifier describes a fabric panel along the front as conveying accessibility and friendliness; its 2023 citation for the Air Performer 7000 and 8000 Series describes the monolithic design where all functional elements are concealed. Both are colleagues’ products, carrying the archetype and the material decision he set.
  • Global Domestic Appliances, 2019Served as one of the global lead designers for the Global Domestic Appliances Product Design Language System in 2019. The system carried into the Versuni-era portfolio from 2021.
  • Physical and digital under one authorshipNamed as creator or designer in a Malaysia design registration for graphical user interface designs for air purifier products, held by Versuni Holding B.V. He is named the same way on the physical purifiers in that same family of registrations. At Philips the interface belongs to product design. That is why his name is on both.
