The family that followed

Founding lineage

Fresh Air Mask programme products received iF Design Awards 2020 and 2021, DFA Design for Asia Awards 2020 and 2021, and Red Dot Design Award 2021, credited to Philips Experience Design. The Fresh Air Mask Lite received Red Dot Best of the Best 2022. These launched after my March 2020 departure: they are the lineage of the programme I founded, not my authorship of those variants. The Kids Fresh Air Mask is the first-generation design at a smaller scale, and the Lite is a minimal restructuring of it — the soft shell removed, the battery moved into the fan module. What they inherit is the product architecture I designed; the variants themselves are the work of the colleagues who made them.

Kohler — water experiences

Senior designer

Water-experience products built on human-factor research, including the Rejuvenation Shower developed with Shanghai University of Traditional Chinese Medicine, and first-generation point-of-use water filtration. That filtration strategy asked what the right user interaction is for a category that did not yet have one, and produced the joystick control language on the KUMIN instant desktop water heater — a showering and bathing gesture brought onto a water heater for the first time. The joystick on top became a recognisable part of the product language for Kohler Kitchen & Bath, not merely a control panel.

About

I am an industrial and product designer. My practice is grounded in physical product development, CMF and prototyping, human-factor research and design-language-system leadership. Over twenty years it has grown to take in the whole experience of the products I design, across hardware, software and human interaction.

The through-line is opening new value spaces — wellness at Kohler, future mobility at BMW, health-tech venture creation at Philips, software-defined vehicles at McLaren — and defining what an integrated product should be and how a design and engineering organisation aligns to build it.

Alongside my work I keep a small private UK design-research practice.
